software · Autodesk clones · Update

First Adobe, then Microsoft. Now Autodesk.

In October 2026 the agent-built copying wave reached engineering software. CADCraft is an open-source AutoCAD look-alike from the ArtCraft team. SolidCraft is a separate open-source take on Fusion. Both are written in pure Rust, both can be driven by an AI agent over MCP, and both are honest about how far they still have to go. Neither will replace your Autodesk seats this year. What they change is the argument at your next renewal, and where software vendors will have to defend themselves now that copying the code is no longer the hard part.

01 · What happened

Two CAD copies in two weeks.

The ArtCraft team spent the first two weeks of October releasing open-source copies of thirteen flagship desktop apps. Most were Adobe and Microsoft products; the CEO briefing covers the full list. Autodesk was on it from the start, as CADCraft, an AutoCAD-style 2D and 3D drafting app. On 13 October a second project, SolidCraft, launched on Hacker News aimed at Fusion, Autodesk's parametric 3D modeller. With both out, Autodesk joins Adobe and Microsoft as a vendor whose flagship products have a free, agent-built look-alike.

  • CADCraft (github.com/storytold/cadcraft) is built by the ArtCraft team, the people behind PhotoCraft. It targets AutoCAD 2027, opens DWG and DXF files, and runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, FreeBSD and in the browser. We counted 6,000 stars and 2,600 forks on 19 October.
  • SolidCraft (github.com/bherbruck/solidcraft, earlier called SolveCraft) is a parametric 3D CAD app with sketches, constraints, a feature timeline, assemblies and sheet metal. It comes from a different developer. How closely it is tied to ArtCraft is unclear. Its agent instructions call CADCraft its “closest sibling”, and its app ID starts with ArtCraft's parent company name, ai.storyteller.
  • The same recipe as the others. Both are written in Rust with the egui and wgpu libraries, dual-licensed MIT or Apache-2.0, and ship a headless CLI and an MCP server. Both carry CLAUDE.md files telling coding agents to work autonomously. CADCraft's roadmap estimates about 50 Opus 5.5 hours to alpha. SolidCraft's estimates about 82 agent hours.

What this is not

Nobody has copied Revit, Civil 3D, Inventor, Vault or Autodesk Construction Cloud. Those are the products that hold most of Autodesk's construction and engineering business. So far the copies cover the desktop drafting tool and the hobbyist-to-prosumer 3D modeller, the two Autodesk products whose value is mostly in one app on one machine. We found no public response from Autodesk as of 19 October.

The most interesting data point is a rover

A community member built a planetary rover model in SolidCraft entirely through the MCP server: 3,323 timeline steps, all rebuilding without errors. That is the real novelty. Whether or not SolidCraft ever matches Fusion feature for feature, an agent can already build a parametric model step by step, measure it and export STEP or 3MF, with no licence and nothing leaving the machine.

03 · Why CAD is harder

CAD's iceberg is deeper than Photoshop's.

The iceberg applies to every Craft app: the menus, the look and the feature checklist sit above the waterline and are cheap to copy. CAD has more mass below the waterline than any other category on the list, because a drawing is a legal and engineering record, not just a picture.

Below the line

DWG has no open spec

Every non-Autodesk DWG reader is reverse-engineered: the Open Design Alliance's libraries, the GPL-licensed LibreDWG, and now acadrust. A drawing that loses its xrefs, custom objects or proxy entities when someone saves it is worse than no drawing at all.

Below the line

The geometry kernel

Fillets, booleans and shells that fail on awkward geometry are where CAD tools earn their price. SolidCraft builds on the Apache-licensed truck kernel and adds its own error-recovery layers. A sibling project, solidcore, is porting NVIDIA's SMLib kernel to Rust. Commercial kernels have had decades of hardening.

Below the line

Drawings and standards

In engineering, the 2D drawing is the contract: dimensions, tolerances, title blocks, layer standards, plot styles. SolidCraft has put drawings off. CADCraft drops anything it doesn't model when it saves.

Below the line

Twenty years of customisation

AutoLISP routines, .NET and ObjectARX add-ins, office templates, block libraries and vertical toolsets. CADCraft rates its automation ecosystem at 10%. Most firms don't know how much of this they rely on until it stops working.

Below the line

The rest of the Autodesk estate

Revit and BIM, Civil 3D, Vault document management, the Construction Cloud and the APIs behind it. A firm that runs on these is not buying AutoCAD on its own, so replacing AutoCAD on its own saves less than it looks.

Below the line

Accountability

Professional indemnity insurers, client contracts and municipal submissions assume a known toolchain and a vendor to call. An open-source project with 300+ open issues and no indemnity is a hard sell to a firm that signs off structures.

04 · What it means for software

When the code is cheap, the moat moves.

Copyright protects code, not what the code does. That has been settled law for decades. What's new is that agents make rebuilding the behaviour cheap. Every vendor whose value sits in one desktop app now has to defend that value somewhere else. These are the places left to defend it, and how well each one holds against a clean-room copy.

LeverWhat it protectsAgainst a clean-room copy
CopyrightSource code, icons, fonts, help text, templatesWeak. In the US, Lotus v. Borland (1995) held that a menu hierarchy is a method of operation, not copyrightable expression. In the EU, SAS Institute v. World Programming (CJEU, 2012) held that a program's functionality, programming language and data file formats aren't protected. Both projects forbid copying Autodesk fonts, hatch patterns, linetypes, icons and help text for exactly this reason.
File formatsDWG, RVT, F3D: the files your work is stored inStrong, and the real moat. Formats aren't copyrightable, but an undocumented format costs time to reverse-engineer and is never quite finished. Autodesk has defended DWG before. In 2006 it sued the Open Design Alliance over DWG files its libraries wrote that carried Autodesk's “TrustedDWG” watermark, and won a restraining order. The 2010 settlement made the ODA drop DWG from its product names, but explicitly let it keep building software that reads and writes DWG files.
TrademarksProduct names, look-alike brandingNarrow, but it's where fights start. It stops “AutoCAD” in a project name or a confusing logo, not the features. Expect renames before lawsuits.
PatentsSpecific inventions in how a feature worksPatchy. A clean room does nothing against a valid patent, because patents cover the method, not the code. Autodesk has a large portfolio. This is the lever most likely to remove individual features from a copy.
ContractWhat licensees may do with the softwareDepends where you are. Autodesk's terms forbid reverse engineering except where the law allows it. In the US, courts have enforced such clauses (Bowers v. Baystate, 2003). In the EU, the Software Directive lets a lawful user observe, study and test a program, and decompile it for interoperability, and a contract can't take that away. That right covers whoever builds the copy, not your firm when it uses it.
Cloud and ecosystemCollaboration, data, APIs, AI features, partner add-insStrongest, and growing. Nothing that runs on Autodesk's servers can be copied by watching the desktop app. Expect more value, and more AI, to move there. CADCraft rates its collaboration and cloud layer at 5%.

The pattern for every big software vendor

Adobe, Microsoft and Autodesk now face the same pressure. The desktop app is cheaper to copy, so value will be defended in formats, cloud services, bundles and contracts. As a buyer, expect three things: more features that only work signed in to the vendor's cloud, bundles that make dropping single products unattractive, and closer attention to licence terms and compliance. None of these is a reason to panic. Each is a reason to read your agreement before renewal rather than at it.

05 · Licensing options

What changes at your Autodesk renewal.

Autodesk has sold subscriptions only, per named user, for years. It has also been moving its reseller sales to a model where you buy from Autodesk and the partner acts as its agent. Free copies don't change those terms. What they change is your fallback option, and that is what a renewal negotiation turns on.

User groupToday's optionWhat the copies add
Viewers and markupFree Autodesk viewers, or a full seat they barely useLittle new. Free viewers already exist. The saving is in finding people on full seats who only view.
Light 2D draftingAutoCAD LT, or one of the existing DWG-based alternatives (BricsCAD, DraftSight, ZWCAD, all built on ODA libraries)A free, open-source option to pilot, but it saves only to R2000 and has no tested AutoCAD round trip. For now it's leverage, not a replacement.
Production draftingFull AutoCAD or a toolset bundleNothing yet. Xrefs, blocks, LISP and plot standards are where CADCraft is weakest.
Hobby, prototyping, 3D printingFusion's personal-use tier or a paid seat; FreeCADSolidCraft is a real option for simple parts, with local files and STEP and 3MF export. Watch whether it keeps improving.
BIM and civilRevit, Civil 3D, the AEC CollectionNothing. The open exit here is IFC and openBIM, not a copied app.
Automated outputA seat per machine, or Autodesk's cloud APIsThe nearest-term win. Agents driving a free engine to batch-convert, check or generate drawings and parts, with no seat and nothing leaving your building.

Three traps

  • Installing an alternative doesn't reduce what you owe. Autodesk software still installed without a licence is a compliance finding, whatever else is on the machine. When you cut seats, uninstall the software and keep the evidence.
  • Mid-term exits are rarely free. Seats on multi-year or prepaid terms renew on their own dates. Map renewal dates and notice periods first, or you end up paying for both tools.
  • GitHub stars aren't a negotiating position. A pilot with real drawings, real users and a count of files that broke is. Vendors know the difference.
06 · Decision guide

Pilot and automate. Don't migrate.

Use them whenSkip them when
You want an agent to generate or check parts and drawings on your own machines, through MCP or a CLI.The drawing is a deliverable someone signs: council submissions, construction issue sets, manufacturing drawings.
You're prototyping or 3D printing simple parts and want local files and STEP/3MF export with no account.Your DWGs carry xrefs, custom objects or vertical-toolset entities. CADCraft drops what it doesn't model when it saves.
You're preparing a renewal and need a tested fallback for a light-user group.You run on Revit, Civil 3D or Vault. Nothing here touches them.
You're teaching CAD concepts, or studying how an agent-built codebase is run.You need a vendor to call, indemnity, or behaviour that won't change from one month to the next.
07 · South African context

Dollar-priced drafting seats, rand-priced projects.

Where it lands here

SA architecture practices, engineering consultancies, contractors and municipalities pay for Autodesk seats priced in dollars, while their fees and budgets are in rand. Full AutoCAD lists at roughly US$2,000 a year per named user before the exchange rate. That makes the light-user group the obvious target: estimators, site staff and admin teams on full seats who mostly view, mark up and plot.

Be careful with work you hand to someone else. Consultants exchange DWGs constantly, and a file that comes back with missing xrefs, blocks or layers costs more than a seat. Start with work that stays inside the firm: internal sketches, 3D printing jigs and fixtures, and agent-generated batch output. For BIM, the open way out is IFC and openBIM, which most SA consultants can already export. It is a better exit than any copied app. And the clean-room law cited above is US and EU law. Get local advice before you build a policy on it.
