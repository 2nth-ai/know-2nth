fin · Domain hub

Finance,
on ERP data.

The finance domain of the knowledge tree. It sits downstream of every ERP — reading the ledger, costing and inventory valuation those systems own, and turning them into reporting, planning and compliance output. The opening leaf is financial reporting; the rest of the band follows the same rule: the ERP keeps the truth, finance shapes the view, a human signs the result.

01 · Leaves

What's in the finance branch

One leaf is live today, with the rest of the close-to-compliance chain mapped as it gets built. Each reads from the ERP layer under biz/erp and never writes financial truth back.

Financial reporting
Turning ERP ledger, costing, WIP and inventory valuation into statutory and management reporting — ERP-agnostic, with Evidence and the open BI stack as the layer that complements ERPNext.

02 · How finance connects

Upstream of the ledger, downstream of the warehouse.
